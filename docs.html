Does Nothing

It is doing it right now A room, a cat, a window, and eighteen hours of light moving across the floor

01 · OverviewWhat is Does Nothing

Every other token opens with a roadmap. Four quarters, arrows, a partnership tier, a staking module nobody asked for. This one opens with a cat asleep on the floor and the honest number zero repeated six times.

The green cat took the cap off, put it down next to him, and went to sleep in the sunlight. He has not shipped anything since. That is the entire product and it works perfectly.

Product
One green cat, asleep, in a room with nothing else in it
Roadmap
There isn't one, and that is the only promise being kept
Uptime
Total. Nothing can break if nothing runs
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · The ledgerSix numbers

the ledger ├── features shipped 0 ├── roadmap items 0 ├── partnerships 0 ├── utility 0 ├── things that happened 0 └── minutes he has slept counting

Five of them are zero and will stay zero. The sixth is the only number on the whole site that moves, and it started the second you opened the page. It is also the only one that has ever been true.

03 · The roomEighteen hours

Scrolling does not advance a story, unlock a section or trigger an effect. It moves the sun. Dawn at the top of the page, midday, then late afternoon, then night, and the clock in the corner counts the hours off as the light crosses the floorboards.

Start
05:40, blue morning light, the cap already off
End
23:40, moonlight, the cat curled tighter
Everything else
Unchanged. The room is the same room in all four
